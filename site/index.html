Case study

A reels feed thatnever stutters.

Sport Oasis · iOS & Android
Google MapsReels feedMedia3 · AVPlayerArabic RTL

Sport Oasis helps people find clubs, academies and sports venues near them. Its short-video reels feed was slow and choppy with Flutter's standard video player.

The plugin gave no real control over buffering, so every swipe meant waiting for the next clip to load.

I replaced it with native players, Media3 on Android and the native player on iOS, connected through platform channels. That let me control preloading and caching myself, so the next clips are ready before you swipe.

Profile

Measured the feed and found the video plugin was the bottleneck.

Go native

Built Media3 (Android) and native iOS players, bridged into Flutter.

Preload & cache

Custom preloading and disk caching for the upcoming reels.

Ship

Smooth, instant swiping, like the big social apps.
